Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

The short version: your artwork never leaves your browser. Will It Print? checks and fixes your files entirely on your own device. We never receive, see, or store the files you check.

Who we are

Will It Print? is operated by 1Oh1 Design LLC (dba ONE ZERO ONE), a Connecticut company. You can reach us at info@1oh1designllc.com.

What we don't collect

  • We don't upload, receive, or store your artwork — all file checking and print-ready export happens locally in your browser.
  • We don't require an account, and we don't ask for personal information to use the tool.
  • We don't use cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics trackers.

The limited data that is involved

Using a website still involves a few third-party services. Here's the full list and what each one touches:

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which — like any web host — may automatically log basic technical request data such as your IP address and browser type for security and reliability. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). We load the Archivo typeface from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the font files are requested. No font data identifies you personally to us.
  • Email report form (Formspree). Only if you choose to use the "email me this report" feature, the email address you enter — plus the basic verdict context you submit — is sent through Formspree to deliver it to us. Don't use that feature if you'd rather not share an email.
  • Donations (Ko-fi). Only if you choose to donate, you'll be taken to Ko-fi, whose own privacy policy and payment processing govern that transaction. We never see your payment details.

How we use the email you give us

If you submit your email through the report form, we use it only to send you the report you asked for and, occasionally, related print tips or 1Oh1 Design updates. You can ask us to remove you at any time by emailing info@1oh1designllc.com.

Children

Will It Print? is a professional print tool and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the tool evolves. The effective date above will reflect the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@1oh1designllc.com.
